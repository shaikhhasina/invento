About the Course

About the Course

The 3-Day NDT & Inspection Fundamentals Certification Course is a practical, beginner-friendly program for students and early-career professionals. You will learn the fundamentals of Non-Destructive Testing and industrial inspection, including UT, PT, MT and VT, along with codes & standards, defect identification and inspection reporting.

Every concept is connected to real industrial applications so you understand not just the theory, but how inspection works on the job.

Syllabus

Course Curriculum

A well-structured 3-day program covering theory, practical concepts and real-world applications.

⬇ Download Detailed Brochure
Module 1

Introduction to NDT & Industrial Inspection

What is NDT, importance of QC, applications and terminology.

Module 2

Visual Testing (VT)

Inspection tools, weld appearance and visual discontinuities.

Module 3

Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT)

Principle, surface prep, dwell time, developer and interpretation.

Module 4

Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)

Magnetisation concepts, equipment and surface defect detection.

Module 5

Ultrasonic Testing (UT) Fundamentals

Sound waves, probes, flaw detectors and A-scan basics.

Module 6

Defect Identification

Cracks, porosity, lack of fusion, inclusions and undercut.

Module 7

Codes & Standards

Introduction to ASME, ASTM, ISO and API terminology.

Module 8

Inspection Reporting

Report elements, checklists, traceability and sample formats.

Module 9

Practical Industrial Applications

Welds, fabricated components, pipelines and case discussions.

Module 10

Career Guidance & Assessment

Career pathways, employer skills, resume, interview and final Q&A.
